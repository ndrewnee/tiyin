Privacy policy

Tiyin is an Android app that builds your spending from the SMS of Uzbek banks and shows it by month and category. This page explains what data the app uses, where it is kept and what part of it leaves your phone.

Effective from 25 November 2026.

What the app reads

SMS. With your permission, Tiyin reads the SMS on your phone, both the ones you already have and new ones. It keeps only messages from HUMO, UZCARD, Kapitalbank (including its short number 2212), Asakabank and Payme. All other SMS are skipped and not saved. Messages with confirmation codes are never saved, even when they come from a bank.

Payme exports. If you open a Payme export file (xlsx) in Tiyin yourself, the app reads its operations: the date, amount, recipient, organization, Payme category, and the card number and card name exactly as the export shows them.

Your edits. The categories and operation types you change by hand, and the language you choose.

Where the data is kept

All of this data is kept only in your phone’s storage, in the app’s database. Tiyin has no server and no accounts, so nothing is uploaded anywhere and nothing is sold. The app has no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking libraries.

What goes online

The app makes one kind of network request, for shops it does not recognize. When a purchase ends up in “Other” and you have not changed its category yourself, the app looks up the shop name, as written in the SMS or export, on the public website orginfo.uz (a directory of organizations in Uzbekistan) and picks a category from the type of business it finds.

Only the shop name is sent, over a secure HTTPS connection. Amounts, dates, card numbers, balances, the names of people you send money to and the text of your SMS are never sent. The answer is saved on the phone so the same name is not looked up twice. orginfo.uz sees the request, and its own rules apply on its side.

Android backups

Tiyin does not turn off Android’s standard backup. If backup to Google is on for your phone, Android may save the app’s database and settings in your Google account and restore them when you reinstall the app. These backups are kept under Google’s rules, and the developer has no access to them. You can turn backup off in your phone’s settings.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

Your data is kept for as long as the app is installed. To delete it, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings (usually “Apps → Tiyin → Storage → Clear data”).

The developer cannot see or delete your data because the developer never has it. A backup in Google is deleted by Google under its own rules, or by you in your Google account settings.

Permissions

Reading and receiving SMS (READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS) lets the app find bank messages. You do not have to grant it: you can continue without SMS, import only Payme exports, and turn SMS access on later. Internet access (INTERNET) is used only to look up shops on orginfo.uz.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about children.

Changes
